Arena floor textures (road012b, concrete047a, ground097, ground111, ground079s) are from ambientCG
(https://ambientcg.com), released under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal licence. The ambient
occlusion map was multiplied into each colour map for the game.

The floor layers layer_gravel_* (from Ground105) and layer_mud_* (from Ground031) are also made from ambientCG
textures under CC0 1.0: resized, the colour rebalanced, and packed (colour + height, normal + roughness) by
tools/textures/make_ground.py. layer_detail.png and layer_macro.png are generated by the same script.
